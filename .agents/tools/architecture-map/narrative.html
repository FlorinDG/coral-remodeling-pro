Since 28 Sep — what reached production

The incident closed (INC-1), and develop → main is separated again: every green commit is promoted on its own SHA after CI and the Vercel preview pass. Ranges that contain a migration are pushed by Florin.

  • WorkHub work orders (WO-1…3). A shift opens as a tabbed work order. The client signs once per work order per day, which freezes the evidence and locks it. Clock-in and clock-out record an address and the distance to the site, and never block (GEO-1). Late entries now land at the correct time (LATE-TZ-1).
  • Timesheets → money (TS-INV-1/2, TS-ARCH-1, HOURS-FMT-1). Four hour statuses. Selected hours become a draft invoice. Settled hours can be archived. Hours show as decimals with HH:mm, in the app and in the export.
  • Documents (DOC-LOCK-1, OCC-MERGE-1). A sent, accepted or rejected quote is locked; Revise creates -vN. A save conflict merges field by field instead of losing the edit.
  • Library (ART-DISC-1, GRID-NUM-1). Discount is a real discount on the canonical net-cost formula. Grid number cells no longer store [object Object]; the data repair has been run.
  • Platform. Fonts are self-hosted (FONTS-1). Belgian dates in 38 places (LOC-SWEEP-1). The date picker works inside dialogs. The coder works plan-first, and every test needs a throw proof.
